About Our School

Excellence in Education Since 1989

Our History & Mission

Parkvale Primary School was established in 1989 with a vision to provide quality education in a nurturing environment. For over 3 decades, we have been committed to developing well-rounded students who excel academically and contribute positively to their community.

Our Educational Philosophy

  • Student-centered learning approaches
  • Safe and inclusive environment for all
  • Development of critical thinking skills
  • Partnership between school and parents
  • Holistic development of each child

School Facilities

Our campus features modern classrooms, a well-stocked library, sports fields, and multipurpose rooms. We continuously upgrade our facilities to provide the best learning environment for our students.

Our Teaching Staff

Our qualified and experienced teachers are dedicated to providing personalized attention to each student. We maintain moderate class sizes to ensure every child receives the support they need to succeed.
