Admissions Process

Step 1: Inquiry

Contact our admissions office or submit an online inquiry form

Step 2: Bank Transfer

Transfer fees to school bank account (details given by admin office by call or inquery form reply)

Step 3: Application

Complete the enrollment application and submit required documents

Step 4: Enrollment

Finalize enrollment and prepare for the first day of school

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate
  • Previous School Records (if applicable)
  • Immunization Records / Clinic card
  • Bank slip of transfer statement (physical or emailed)
  • Parent/Guardian Identification
